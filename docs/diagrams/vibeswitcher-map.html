VibeSwitcher: who talks to whom

The app on your Mac sits between you and the agents it starts: the lead, workers and verifiers run caged in terminals the session host owns and reach the rest only through VibeSwitcher's tools, the helper is a one-shot model call with no tools, and nothing leaves for GitHub or a wallet until you click.

  • The core: plain code, no model
  • A door: the one way through to something outside
  • A caged agent: a session with deny rules, reaching out only through VibeSwitcher (the helper: a headless call with no tools)
  • Only your click
