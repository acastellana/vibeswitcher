# Improvement backlog

Ranked ideas for making the lead deliver exactly what the user wants: without issues, safely, cheaply, as fast as possible, asking the user only what's necessary. Drawn from the 2026-11-07 review of an agent-harness research atlas (about 100 papers, all verified to exist; several headline numbers were overstated), a self-audit of VS's own records (448 tasks), a red-team pass on the code, Muse's docs, and what went wrong on 2026-11-07. Ranking = expected effect on that goal ÷ cost. **Validate** says what has to be measured or tried before it's built for good. **Status**: idea · queued (a board task) · in progress · done.

VS evidence cited: rerun merges 33–38% of merges; first-audit pass 68%; ~4% escapes; 32% same-model audits since Nov 1; 25% of flaky fixes flaked again; Important findings at 5% for tasks under 0.25 h vs 63% for tasks of 1 h or more; 822 main-thread stalls of 2 s or more since Oct 28; on 2026-11-07 the user found 4 bugs (shell copy, chat selection, Run, dictation) that the system could have caught, and the lead proposed 4 chat features that already existed.
